## What Meta Actually Is

Meta is not a social media company.
It is the world's largest surveillance
advertising operation embedded into
daily civilian life through products
people use to connect with each other.

Advertising accounted for 98% of
Meta's total revenue in 2024 —
$164 billion out of approximately
$164.5 billion total.

One privacy policy.

## The Three Invisible Hooks

These operate across the entire
internet — not just Meta's platforms.

**1. The Meta Pixel** #verified
A small piece of code embedded on
approximately 30% of all websites
on the internet. When you visit
any site carrying the Pixel —
a lawyer's website, a medical page,
a car dealership, a news article —
the Pixel reports your visit to
Meta instantly.

This happens whether you have
a Facebook account or not.
This happens when every Meta app
is closed. This happens when you
think you are browsing privately.

The EFF confirmed: Meta's tracking
tools are embedded in millions of
websites and apps — you cannot
escape Meta's surveillance simply
by avoiding or deleting its apps.

**2. Software Development Kits (SDKs)**
#verified
Many free apps — games, weather apps,
fitness trackers, period trackers —
are built using Meta's free
development tools. The price of
those free tools is data.
Apps built with Meta SDKs report
user behaviour back to Meta including
location data, health metrics,
usage patterns, and device information.

You gave none of these apps
permission to share your data with Meta.
You agreed to it in terms and
conditions written to be unreadable.

**3. Conversions API (CAPI)** #verified
Traditional tracking happens on
your device — you can attempt
to block it with browser settings
or VPNs. CAPI operates server to server.
The retailer's computer talks
directly to Meta's computer
without touching your device.
There is no consumer level
defence against this.

## The End-To-End Encryption Reversal
#verified

Meta announced in March 2026 that
end-to-end encrypted messaging on
Instagram will no longer be supported
after May 8, 2026.

The stated reason: low adoption.
"Very few people were opting in to
end-to-end encrypted messaging in DMs,
so we're removing this option."

The actual story is more layered.
Platformer reported that the low
adoption rate was of Meta's own
creation — the feature was buried
behind four taps and menus and
was never advertised or set as default.
Meta built a feature, hid it,
watched it go unused, then cited
its own hiding of it as justification
for removal.

Matthew Green, cryptographer and
professor at Johns Hopkins University,
flagged publicly that Meta previously
conducted a human rights impact
assessment in 2022 that found
expanding end-to-end encryption
"supports a range of fundamental
human rights." That commitment
is now being reversed.

When Instagram's encryption ends
on May 8, 2026, Meta will regain
the technical ability to scan and
act on the content of every DM —
reopening the door to automated
content scanning, AI training,
ad personalisation, and compliance
with law enforcement requests.

Meta's direction of users toward
WhatsApp for encryption is noted.
WhatsApp message content is encrypted.
Everything else flows to Meta.
See the WhatsApp section below.

## WhatsApp

WhatsApp has 2 billion monthly
active users globally.
Its end-to-end encryption on
messages is genuine and
technically verified. #verified

What is not protected:

**Metadata** — who you talk to,
when, for how long, from where,
how frequently — is fully
harvested by Meta. The content
of your messages is private.
The map of your relationships,
communication patterns, and
social network is not. #verified

**The Accounts Center problem:**
If you link your WhatsApp account
to your Facebook or Instagram
account through Meta's Accounts
Center, Meta explicitly states
it combines data from all linked
accounts to build a more complete
profile of you.
This is in their own privacy policy.
#verified

**Meta AI on WhatsApp:**
As of December 2025, interactions
with Meta AI on WhatsApp are used
to personalise ads across Facebook
and Instagram. If you do not
interact with Meta AI on WhatsApp,
your data is not used for AI
development per Meta's policy.
The opt-out is not interacting
with Meta AI at all. #verified

**The honest position on WhatsApp:**
For private message content:
genuinely protected by encryption.
For everything else — your
relationships, your patterns,
your location, your device —
you are inside the Meta
surveillance ecosystem.
Signal is the alternative.

are now signals in your
advertising profile.

comments section, and direct

naturally type to encourage

## The Pay-For-Privacy Model —
## Ruled Illegal
#verified

In November 2024 Meta introduced
an ad-free subscription service
in the EU: €5.99/month on web,
€7.99 on mobile. Users could
pay to avoid personalised advertising.

In April 2025 the European Commission
formally ruled that this model
violates both GDPR and the Digital
Markets Act. The ruling: the model
does not provide a legally valid
alternative to consent because it
forces users to either pay for
their fundamental right to privacy
or accept invasive surveillance.
Privacy behind a paywall is
not consent. It is coercion.

This is only illegal in the EU.
In the US and most of the world
it remains legal. The model
reveals the valuation Meta places
on your data: a few euros a month.
The actual revenue per user it
generates from your data is
significantly higher. #credible

## Ray-Ban Meta Smart Glasses —
## The Physical World Layer
#verified

Meta's Ray-Ban smart glasses —
wearable cameras with built-in
Meta AI — represent the extension
of Meta's surveillance infrastructure
from the digital world into
physical space.

In October 2024 two Harvard students
published a demonstration showing
that the glasses could be used to
identify strangers on the street
by cross-referencing live camera
feeds with publicly available data —
pulling names, addresses, and phone
numbers from people's faces
within seconds.

The students called the project
I-XRAY. They published it to
demonstrate the risk, not to
deploy it.

The technology they used is not
hidden. It is the combination of
a wearable camera, facial recognition,
and publicly available data —
all accessible to anyone
with the glasses.

This is the physical world layer
of [[The Planetary Nervous System]].

#verified

Meta has been fined under GDPR
more than any other platform.

in violation of fundamental
privacy rights.

practices — the ones ruled
illegal in Europe — operate
freely in the United States
and most of the world.

The legal architecture that
protects Europeans does not
protect you unless you are
in Europe.
