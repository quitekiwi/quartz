## How It Was Built

**Twitter — October 2022:**
Elon Musk acquired Twitter for
$44 billion. Fired approximately
80% of staff. Rebranded to X.
Introduced paid verification —
removing the distinction between
authenticated accounts and anyone
willing to pay $8/month.
Content moderation was drastically
reduced. #verified

A study released in February 2023
analysing thousands of English
language posts found a 50% rise
in hate speech in the first eight
months of Musk's ownership.
No meaningful reduction in bot
accounts — which Musk had
specifically promised to address. #verified

Advertisers fled. By December 2024
Fidelity had slashed its valuation
of X to approximately $12 billion —
a 73% decline from the $44 billion
acquisition price. X's annual
EBITDA stood at approximately
$1.2 billion — roughly equal to
its interest payments. #verified

**xAI — March 2023:**
Musk founded xAI with 11 researchers
recruited from OpenAI, Google DeepMind,
and other institutions. Stated mission:
"maximally truth-seeking AI" as a counter
to what Musk described as politically
correct AI models. #verified

**The merger — March 28, 2025:**
xAI acquired X in an all-stock deal
valuing X at $33 billion — less than
the $44 billion Musk paid for it in 2022.
The new holding company: XAI Holdings Corp.

Musk's announcement: "Today, we
officially take the step to combine
the data, models, compute, distribution
and talent." #verified

**SpaceX acquisition — February 2, 2026:**
SpaceX acquired xAI in an all-stock
transaction valuing SpaceX at $1 trillion
and xAI at $250 billion — combined
total $1.25 trillion. xAI is now
a wholly owned subsidiary of SpaceX.
Following the acquisition half of
xAI's co-founders left. Two more
were pushed out in March 2026 after
an audit by SpaceX and Tesla. #verified

## What The Merger Actually Does

Before the merger Grok was already
training on X user data. The operational
benefit existed before the deal.
The deal was not about operations.

It was about data rights,
legal structure, and power. #credible

X updated its terms of service
in November 2024 — months before
the formal merger — to expand its
rights over user content to include
"training of our machine learning
and artificial intelligence models"
and the right to "upload and download"
user content "for any purpose." #verified

The merger removed the last legal
barriers between user data and
AI training. Every post, every
interaction, every DM on X now
flows without legal friction into
xAI's training pipeline.

600 million monthly active users.
Every post since 2006.
Every image. Every interaction.
Possibly location data.
All of it feeding Grok. #verified

## What X Collects

What X collects from its users: #verified

- All public posts, likes, follows,
  reposts, and interactions
- Private DMs — not encrypted
- Biometric data including face
  and voice recognition
  (added to privacy policy
  September 2023)
- Employment and educational history
  (added September 2023)
- Location data
- Device and network information
- Payment data
- Browsing data through X's
  integrated browser

The FTC charged Twitter in 2022
for using phone numbers and emails
provided for security purposes
to serve targeted advertising
without user consent.
Twitter paid $150 million —
one of the largest FTC privacy
settlements at the time. #verified

## The Colossus Supercomputer

In Memphis, Tennessee xAI has
built Colossus — described as
one of the world's largest
AI computing clusters. #verified

200,000–230,000 Nvidia GPUs.
Power draw in the hundreds
of megawatts.
xAI purchased a third Memphis
building in December 2025 to
expand training capacity to
nearly 2 gigawatts of compute.

In August 2024 — before the
Memphis facility was complete —
Musk was reportedly diverting
Nvidia chips ordered by Tesla
to X and xAI instead.

The power draw of Colossus is
comparable to the Tennessee Valley
Authority's large gas-fired power
plants nearby. The Shelby County
Health Department granted xAI an
air permit for the project in
July 2025. The environmental
implications of running a facility
of this scale on fossil fuel adjacent
infrastructure — by a man who
simultaneously promotes clean energy
through Tesla — is a documented
contradiction. #verified
