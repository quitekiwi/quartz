a signal changes as it bounces off
and passes through objects in a space.

machine learning algorithms,
researchers have demonstrated

learning models.

## WiFi 6 and 7 — Sensing Built In

WiFi is evolving from its initial
802.11 standard through WiFi 6
(802.11ax) to WiFi 7 (802.11be)
released in 2024.

The IEEE 802.11bf amendment —
ratified in 2024 — formally
standardises WiFi sensing as
part of the WiFi specification.
Sensing capability is now a
built-in feature of the standard,
not a research experiment.

The PMC review of WiFi sensing
advancements (November 2024)
states: the IEEE 802.11bf amendment
planned sensing as part of a
regular WiFi service by late 2024.
#verified
