## What Is Not In This Folder

This folder does not contain:

Conspiracy. Every claim is tagged
and sourced. `#verified` means
documented. `#credible` means
well-supported but not fully proven.
`#theory` means reasoning from
evidence. `#investigate` means
flagged for deeper research.

Wellness marketing. The distinction
between this folder and the
alternative health industry is
the tagging system. We do not
claim things work because
someone sells them.
We document what the science
actually shows — including
the limitations.

False hope. Some of what was
suppressed was suppressed because
it did not work. This folder
only documents what has been
confirmed or credibly supported.
The honest counterargument
appears in every note where it exists.

Prescriptions. This folder tells
you what exists and what the
evidence shows. What you do
with it is yours.

None of this is a complete solution.

The managed world is large,
well-resourced, and deeply
embedded in daily infrastructure.

regenerative food is not dismantling
surveillance capitalism.

That is not the point.

The point is the documented
difference between a human being
operating with biological coherence,
awareness, and sovereignty —
and one operating without it.

The first is harder to nudge,
harder to predict, harder to manage.
Not immune. Harder.

The second is the default
product of the system
documented in [[The Managed World]].

The choice between them is
available right now.
It does not require waiting
for institutions to change,
for legislation to pass,
or for the managed world
to acknowledge what it is doing.

It requires deciding that
the counter is worth practising.
