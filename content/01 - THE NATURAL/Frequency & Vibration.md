**Wave-particle duality:**

In 1905 Einstein demonstrated that
light — long understood as a wave —
also behaves as discrete packets
of energy called photons. Particles.

In 1924 Louis de Broglie proposed
the inverse: that electrons and all
matter also exhibit wave properties
with a characteristic wavelength
inversely proportional to momentum.

The mathematical relationship:
λ = h/p
Wavelength equals Planck's constant
divided by momentum.

Within three years Davisson and Germer
experimentally confirmed electron
diffraction — proving de Broglie correct.
He received the Nobel Prize in 1929.

What this means:
Every particle of matter — every atom
every molecule every cell every object
you have ever touched — has a
characteristic wavelength and frequency.
Matter is not just like a wave.
Matter behaves as a wave. #verified
