## Vitamin D — The One Everyone Knows

UVB radiation converts 7-dehydrocholesterol
in skin to vitamin D3 which is
then processed by the liver and
kidneys into the active hormone
calcitriol. #verified

Calcitriol regulates over 2,000 genes —
approximately 10% of the human genome.
It governs: #verified
- Calcium and phosphorus metabolism
- Bone density and formation
- Immune system regulation
- Insulin secretion
- Cardiovascular function
- Neuromuscular function
- Cell differentiation and
  apoptosis (cancer prevention)
- Brain development in utero
  and in early childhood

Vitamin D deficiency is associated
with: depression autoimmune disease
osteoporosis cardiovascular disease
multiple sclerosis type 1 diabetes
and multiple cancers.

Approximately 1 billion people
globally are estimated to be
vitamin D deficient or insufficient.
#verified

**The supplement problem:**

sunlight exposure itself.

The current scientific consensus

operate through multiple pathways
simultaneously — and vitamin D
supplementation only addresses one.
The sun cannot be replaced by
a pill. #verified
