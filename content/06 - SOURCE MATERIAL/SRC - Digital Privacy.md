## VPN Recommendations

**Mullvad VPN**
https://mullvad.net/en
Swedish jurisdiction.
No-logs policy independently audited.
Does not require email or personal
information to create account.
Accepts cash and crypto.

**ProtonVPN**
https://protonvpn.com
Swiss jurisdiction.
Open source. Independently audited.
Free tier available — slower speeds.

**Why not free VPNs:**
Business model requires monetizing
user data — defeating the purpose.
Multiple documented cases of free
VPN providers selling traffic logs.

## Privacy Tools

**uBlock Origin**
https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock
Open source. Free.
Blocks Meta Pixel and most trackers.
Most effective content blocker available.

**O&O ShutUp10++**
https://www.oo-software.com/en/shutup10
Free. No installation required.

https://ollama.com
