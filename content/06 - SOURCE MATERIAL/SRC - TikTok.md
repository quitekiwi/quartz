## Data Collection

**TikTok Privacy Policy — Biometric Update**
June 2, 2021 — "Image and Audio 
Information" section added.
https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/us/privacy-policy/en
Confirms: faceprints, voiceprints, 
keystroke patterns.

**Carnegie Mellon — Alessandro Acquisti**
TIME Magazine, June 14, 2021
https://time.com/6071773/tiktok-faceprints-voiceprints-privacy/
Statement on permanent nature of 
biometric identifiers and 
re-identification risk.

**$92M Biometric Settlement — 2021**
Illinois Biometric Information 
Privacy Act (BIPA) class action.
Covers data practices up to 
October 13, 2022.

**Ongoing Lawsuit — Post 2022**
ByteDance motion to dismiss filed 
May 16, 2025.
ID Tech Wire: 
https://idtechwire.com/bytedance-moves-to-dismiss-tiktok-biometric-privacy-lawsuit-in-us-courts/

## The Ban and Sale

**Supreme Court Ruling — January 17, 2025**
TikTok Inc. v. Garland, 604 U.S. 56 (2025)
Full opinion: 
https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/24-656_ca7d.pdf
Unanimous. Data collection cited as 
primary constitutional justification.

**The Sale — Closed January 22, 2026**
Wikipedia — TikTok Inc. v. Garland:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok,_Inc._v._Garland
New entity: TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC
Owners: Oracle, Silver Lake, MGX
ByteDance retains algorithm initially.

**Protecting Americans from Foreign 
Adversary Controlled Applications Act**
Signed April 24, 2024.
congress.gov — full text available.

not volume.
