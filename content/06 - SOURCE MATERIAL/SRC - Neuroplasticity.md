## Chronic Stress and Brain Structure

**PMC — Stress Effects on Neuronal
Structure: Hippocampus, Amygdala,
Prefrontal Cortex**
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4677120/
Chronic stress causes CA3 neuron
dendritic retraction confirmed.
Reversible nature of chronic
stress-induced changes confirmed.
Sedentary older adults walking 1h/day
for 6-12 months showed hippocampal
formation enlargement confirmed
(Erickson et al. 2011).
Exercise-stimulated dentate gyrus
neurogenesis confirmed.
Hippocampal volume increases with
intense learning confirmed
(Draganski et al. 2006).
Circadian disruption causes dendritic
atrophy and cognitive impairment confirmed.

**PMC — Stress Effects on Hippocampus:
Critical Review**
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4561403/
PTSD patients had smaller hippocampal
volumes correlating with verbal
memory deficits confirmed.
Prolonged cortisol exposure reduces
neurogenesis and impairs synaptic
plasticity in hippocampus confirmed.
Epigenetic mechanisms in chronic
cortisol elevation confirmed.

**PMC — Chronic Stress and
Hippocampal Vulnerability**
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2746750/
Meta-analysis of 23 PTSD studies
confirms hippocampal volume differences
covary with PTSD severity confirmed.
Antidepressant treatment can increase
hippocampal volumes in PTSD
patients confirmed.
Reversibility of hippocampal volume
suggests structural recovery
is possible confirmed.
Dendritic retraction may persist
weeks, months or years — Glucocorticoid
Vulnerability Hypothesis documented.

**PMC — Stress-Related Dysfunction
of Adult Hippocampal Neurogenesis**
https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/22/14/7339
Elevated glucocorticoid levels lead to
downregulation of adult neurogenesis confirmed.
Adult neurogenesis may protect against
stress-induced depression as
resilience mechanism confirmed.
BDNF serum levels reflect BDNF
brain levels and correlate with
mood disorders confirmed.
Stress significantly suppresses BDNF
in hippocampus confirmed.

**PMC — Role of Adult Hippocampal
Neurogenesis in Stress Resilience**
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4721321/
Chronic stress decreases all stages
of adult hippocampal neurogenesis confirmed.
Effect reversible by chronic
antidepressant treatment confirmed.
Environmental enrichment beneficial
for reversing stress-induced
behavioural changes confirmed.

**Brain Health University — 2025**

## BDNF — Key Mechanism

Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF):

- Supports neuronal survival and growth
- Facilitates new synapse formation
- Upregulated by aerobic exercise
- Suppressed by chronic stress
- Serum levels used as biomarker
  for mood disorders
  Referenced across multiple sources above. #verified
