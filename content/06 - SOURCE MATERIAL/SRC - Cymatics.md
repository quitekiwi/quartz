## Chladni Figures — Historical Confirmation

**Ernst Chladni (1787)**
"Entdeckungen über die Theorie
des Klanges" (Discoveries on the
Theory of Sound)
Leipzig: Weidmanns Erben und Reich.
First scientific documentation of
sound-induced geometric patterns
on vibrating plates.
Predates Jenny by 180 years.
Confirms the physics is not new. #verified
