## What It Was

In 1975 the Church Committee —
a United States Senate select committee
investigating intelligence agency abuses
— held public hearings on CIA activities.

During these hearings CIA Director
William Colby was confronted with
a dart gun developed by the CIA's
Technical Services Division.

The weapon: #verified
- Modified Colt M1911 pistol —
  battery powered, electrically fired
- Fired a dart the width of a human hair
- The dart was made of frozen saxitoxin
  — shellfish toxin — suspended in water
- It penetrated skin and dissolved instantly
- It caused cardiac arrest by shutting
  down the nervous system
- It left only a small red puncture mark
- The toxin degraded rapidly in the body
- Standard autopsies of the era
  would not detect it
- The death would be ruled a natural
  heart attack
- Effective range: up to 100 metres
- Nearly silent operation

Saxitoxin is a Schedule 1 Chemical
Warfare Agent. Its lethal dose is
0.57mg. The CIA's Technical Services
Division — led by Dr. Nathan Gordon
at Fort Detrick — discovered that
freezing the toxin in water created
a dart that could be injected through
skin and leave no traceable evidence.

This was shown on live television.
The weapon was placed on a table
in the Senate chamber.
Senator Frank Church and Senator
John Tower held it for the cameras.
It is in the Congressional Record.
It is in the US Capitol Visitor Center's
official photographic archive. #verified
