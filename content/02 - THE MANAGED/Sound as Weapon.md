## Infrasound — The Honest Account

Infrasound is the most overclaimed
topic in acoustic weapons research.

separating what the physics shows
from what the popular narrative claims.

**What is verified:** #verified

  hypothermia and tissue shearing

A 2025 PMC narrative review

**The honest counterargument:** #credible

sufficiently high infrasound
amplitude at a minimum specified range."

Military Medicine published a review

for use as a nonlethal weapon."
