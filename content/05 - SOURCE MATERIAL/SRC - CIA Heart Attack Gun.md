September 16–17, 1975**
Senate Select Committee to Study
Governmental Operations with Respect
to Intelligence Activities.
CIA Director William Colby presented
the weapon — a modified Colt M1911
pistol — to the committee in person.
Senators Frank Church and John Tower
publicly displayed the weapon.
News cameras present. Ran nationwide.
This is in the Congressional Record.

## The Weapon — Technical Details

**Military.com — Documented Overview**
https://www.military.com/history/cias-heart-attack-gun-cold-war-weapon-targeted-assassinations.html
Confirms:
- Modified Colt M1911 pistol
- Battery-powered, electrically fired
- Fired frozen dart width of human hair
- Dart: saxitoxin (shellfish toxin)
  frozen in water
- Dart penetrated skin and dissolved
- Left only small red puncture wound
- Saxitoxin degraded rapidly in body
- Standard autopsies would not detect it
- Death would be ruled natural heart attack
- Effective range: up to 100 meters
- Scope attachment available
- Nearly silent operation

**SOFREP — Saxitoxin Documentation**
https://sofrep.com/news/death-by-ice-dart-the-cias-heart-attack-gun/
Confirms: Saxitoxin (STX) is Schedule 1
Chemical Warfare Agent. Lethal dose:
0.57mg if ingested. Shuts down nervous
system. Death by respiratory failure
in minutes.

## Key People

**Dr. Nathan Gordon — Fort Detrick**
CIA researcher. Discovered mixing
toxin with water and freezing created
injectable dart. Directed development
of delivery system.
Notably: retained 5.9 grams of
shellfish toxin — nearly one third of
all ever produced — in Washington DC
storage despite Nixon's 1969 order
to destroy all biological weapons.
Church Committee blasted the lapse.

**Mary Embree — CIA Technical Services**
Former CIA researcher. Confirmed
publicly she was ordered to find
a poison that would cause a heart
attack undetectable in autopsy.
Her research led to saxitoxin
selection. Confirmed weapon tested
on animals and prisoners.

**Dr. Sidney Gottlieb — MKUltra Director**
Head of Project MKUltra.
Heart attack gun developed under
companion program MKNAOMI —
CIA-Army Chemical Corps collaboration
focused on chemical and biological
weapons for clandestine use.

## The Program Context

**MKNAOMI**
CIA-Army Chemical Corps joint program.
Developed alongside MKUltra.
Focused on chemical, biological,
and radiological materials for
clandestine operations.
Shut down by Nixon in 1970.
Toxin stockpiles secretly retained
by Gordon despite shutdown order.

**KGB Precedent**
KGB operative Bohdan Stashynsky
used similar crude poison dart weapon
to assassinate two targets — 1957
and 1959 — before defecting.
His confession was the trigger for
CIA development of more sophisticated
version. Referenced in multiple
documented sources including Military.com.

## What Was Never Confirmed

**Operational use:**
Whether the weapon was ever used
on a human target has never been
confirmed in declassified records.
CIA Director Colby stated in 1975:
"We are aware that that operation
was not in fact completed" regarding
one referenced operation.

**Medium article — honest assessment:**
https://medium.com/@zerosignalpodcast/the-cias-heart-attack-gun-what-we-really-know-and-dont-543b4ab77107
"The most sensational claim — that
the device reliably caused fatal
heart attacks that autopsies couldn't
detect — has never been confirmed by
declassified records. It's plausible
in the abstract but there's no official
public evidence tying the device to
confirmed murders."

This honest caveat belongs in
the vault. The weapon existed.
Its operational use is unconfirmed.

## CIA Assassination Program Context

Church Committee confirmed CIA
maintained active assassination
program targeting foreign leaders:
- Patrice Lumumba (Congo)
- Rafael Trujillo (Dominican Republic)
- Ngo Dinh Diem (Vietnam)
- René Schneider (Chile)
- Fidel Castro (Cuba — multiple attempts)

The heart attack gun was developed
in this operational context.
Whether it was the specific tool
used in any of these operations
is not confirmed in declassified records.

## The Saxitoxin Stockpile

President Nixon's 1969 executive order
banned US biological weapons programs
and ordered all toxins destroyed.
Gordon secretly retained 5.9 grams
of saxitoxin and cobra venom
in Washington DC storage —
in defiance of a direct presidential order.
Discovered by Church Committee in 1975.

5.9 grams of saxitoxin.
Lethal dose: 0.57mg.
That stockpile could have killed
over 10,000 people.
Retained illegally. Discovered by accident.
