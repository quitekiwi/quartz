## THz Safety — Honest Counterpoint

**Huawei 6G Research**
"Terahertz Sensing and Communication
Towards Future Intelligence
Connected Networks"
https://www.huawei.com/en/huaweitech/future-technologies/terahertz-sensing-communication
States: THz photons non-ionizing.
1 THz = 4 meV — far below
ionization thresholds.
Industry safety position confirmed.

**ICNIRP Guidelines**
International Commission on
Non-Ionizing Radiation Protection
Current guidelines address
thermal effects only.
Non-thermal biological effects
not incorporated into standards.
icnirp.org
