## Primary Works

**Russell, W. — The Universal One (1926)**
First publication of Russell Cosmogony.
Contains original periodic charts.
University of Science and Philosophy.
The book Russell sent to 125 world
scientists in 1926.

**Russell, W. — The Secret of Light (1947)**
Walter Russell Foundation.
Revised cosmological framework.
Contains revised periodic charts.
Most complete statement of
Russell's unified theory.

**Russell, W. — The Message of the
Divine Iliad (1948–49)**
Two volumes. Walter Russell Foundation.

**Technetium Discovery — 1937**
Element 43. First element
confirmed to have no stable isotopes.
Discovered by Perrier and Segrè.
Russell left a gap for it in 1926.
Eleven year gap confirmed.

**Transuranic Elements**

first synthetic transuranic.
Created 1940 — McMillan and Abelson.
