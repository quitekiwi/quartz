## Gerald Pollack — Primary Sources

**Pollack, G.H. —
The Fourth Phase of Water:
Beyond Solid, Liquid, and Vapor (2013)**
Ebner and Sons Publishers, Seattle.
Primary popular science text.
Describes EZ water, fourth phase,
and biological implications.
Note: This is NOT a peer reviewed
scientific monograph. Pollack
acknowledges the layered structure
proposal is speculative.

**Pollack, G.H. —
Cells, Gels and the Engines
of Life (2001)**
Ebner and Sons, Seattle.
Earlier work establishing cell water
as gel-like and ordered.

**Zheng, J. & Pollack, G.H. (2003)**
"Long-range forces extending from
polymer-gel surfaces"
Physical Review E, 68, 031408.
First peer reviewed description
of the exclusion zone phenomenon.
Confirmed: microspheres repelled
from hydrophilic surfaces.

**Wang, A. & Pollack, G.H. (2024)**
"Exclusion-zone water inside and
outside of plant xylem vessels"
Scientific Reports, Vol. 14,
Article 12071.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-62983-3
Most recent peer reviewed publication.
EZ water in plant vessels confirmed.

**Chai, B. & Pollack, G.H. (2010)**
"Solute-free interfacial zones
in polar liquids"
Journal of Physical Chemistry B,
114, 5371–5375.
EZ phenomenon in non-water
polar liquids confirmed.

**Pollack Lab — University of Washington**
https://www.pollacklab.org/research
Official lab page.
Confirms: EZ water fourth phase,
infrared energy building EZ,
electrical charge separation.

**Pollack — TEDx Guelph University**
"The Fourth Phase of Water"
Accessible non-specialist overview.

**Prigogine Medal 2012**
Awarded to Pollack for contributions
to thermodynamics.
Institutional recognition confirmed.

## Critical Review — Must Read

**Elton, D.C. et al. (2020)**
"Exclusion Zone Phenomena in Water —
A Critical Review of Experimental
Findings and Theories"
International Journal of Molecular
Sciences, 21(14): 5041.
PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7404113/
Full text: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.06822

Key findings:
- EZ phenomenon confirmed and
  reproduced by multiple groups
- Pollack's H₃O₂ structural
  explanation NOT in peer reviewed
  publications — only in his book
- Schurr diffusiophoresis theory
  presents compelling alternative
  explanation for core EZ phenomenon
- Neutron radiography does NOT
  support higher density phase
- EZ phenomenon real.
  Structural explanation contested.

**UNSW Chemistry Professor —
The Conversation (2022, updated 2025)**
"Don't fall for the snake oil
claims of structured water"
https://theconversation.com/dont-fall-for-the-snake-oil-claims-of-structured-water-a-chemist-explains-why-its-nonsense-188159

Key points:
- H₃O₂ formula not in peer reviewed
  publication — book only
- Simple arithmetic: converting H₂O
  to H₃O₂ would release H₂ gas —
  not observed
- EZ phenomenon interesting and real
- Commercial structured water
  products tested with NMR show
  no difference from pure water
- Pollack distances himself from
  most commercial products
- Pollack has his own structured
  water company — conflict of
  interest worth noting

**Important distinction for the vault:**
EZ phenomenon = #verified (reproduced)
H₃O₂ structural explanation = not peer
reviewed, alternative explanations exist
Commercial structured water = snake oil
not supported by Pollack's own
peer reviewed work

## EZ Water and Cell Biology

**PLOS ONE — Sharma, Pollack et al. (2018)**
"Exclusion zone and heterogeneous
water structure at ambient temperature"
https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0195057
Confirmed: three dimensional
cell-like structured exclusion zone
in water prepared by two methods.

**Ball, P. (2017)**
"Water is an active matrix of life
for cell and molecular biology"
Proceedings of the National Academy
of Sciences, 114: 13327–13335.
Confirms: interfacial water near
cell membranes behaves differently
from bulk water. Mainstream
peer reviewed support for
the general principle.
