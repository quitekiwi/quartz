## Grounding / Earthing — Primary Research

**Oschman, Chevalier & Brown (2015)**
"The Effects of Grounding on Inflammation, 
Immune Response, Wound Healing, and 
Prevention and Treatment of Chronic 
Inflammatory and Autoimmune Diseases"
Journal of Inflammation Research
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4378297/
Key finding: Earth contact produces 
measurable changes in white blood cells, 
cytokines, and inflammatory markers.

**Chevalier et al. (2012)**
"Earthing: Health Implications of 
Reconnecting the Human Body to the 
Earth's Surface Electrons"
Journal of Environmental and Public Health
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3265077/
Key finding: Reduced pain, better sleep, 
ANS shift from sympathetic to 
parasympathetic. Randomised controlled trial.

**Chevalier et al. (2013)**
"Earthing the Human Body Reduces 
Blood Viscosity"
Journal of Alternative and 
Complementary Medicine
Key finding: Grounding increases red 
blood cell surface charge, reducing 
clumping and cardiovascular risk.

**Jamieson, I.A. (2023)**
"Grounding as Related to 
Electromagnetic Hygiene: 
An Integrative Review"
Biomedical Journal
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10105031/
Key finding: Insulating footwear, 
high-rise living, elevated beds 
proposed as contributing factors 
to chronic disease in modern societies.

**Feynman, R. — Lectures on Physics**
Nobel Prize winner's description 
of body electrical potential 
equalisation with Earth's surface. 
The theoretical foundation for 
why grounding works electrically.

**Menigoz et al. (2020)**
"Integrative and Lifestyle Medicine 
Strategies Should Include Earthing"
Explore (journal)
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32007537/
Clinical review recommending earthing 
as standard integrative medicine practice.
